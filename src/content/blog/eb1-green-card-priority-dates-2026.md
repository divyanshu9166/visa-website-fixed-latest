# EB-1 Green Card in 2026: Priority Dates, EB-1A Eligibility, and the August Visa Bulletin

The **EB-1 green card** is the first-preference employment-based category — the fastest route to a U.S. green card for top-tier talent. In 2026, EB-1 remains **current for most of the world**, but demand from China and India is tightening the timeline fast. This guide breaks down the current **EB-1 priority dates**, the three EB-1 subcategories, and what the August 2026 Visa Bulletin means for your case.

> **Bottom line:** Per the August 2026 Visa Bulletin, EB-1 Final Action Dates are **Current** for all countries except **China (July 1, 2023)** and **India (October 15, 2022)**. The State Department has warned that **EB-1 India could become unavailable** in the coming weeks due to high demand. Confirm your priority date and chargeability before filing.

## What the EB-1 green card is

EB-1 is the **first-preference employment-based** immigrant category, receiving **28.6% of the worldwide employment-based** limit each year, plus any numbers unused by the fourth and fifth preferences. It is designed for individuals at the top of their field and has three subcategories:

- **EB-1A — Extraordinary Ability:** For individuals with sustained national or international acclaim. **No job offer and no labor certification (PERM) required** — you can **self-petition** with Form I-140.
- **EB-1B — Outstanding Professors and Researchers:** Requires an employer and evidence of international recognition; no labor certification.
- **EB-1C — Multinational Managers and Executives:** For managers/executives transferred to a U.S. entity; employer-sponsored, no labor certification.

Because EB-1A allows self-petition and skips PERM, it is often the fastest employment-based path — when a visa number is available.

## How EB-1 compares to EB-2 in 2026

The pressure on employment-based categories is not limited to EB-1. In the same August 2026 bulletin, **EB-2 India is listed as "Unavailable" (U)** for the remainder of the fiscal year, with processing expected to resume when the new fiscal year begins in October 2026. That backlog is a major reason many India-born applicants with strong credentials pursue **EB-1A self-petitions** — a current or near-current EB-1 date can be years ahead of EB-2 or EB-3.

## Filing steps and where to track timelines

1. **Confirm your priority date** — the date your I-140 (or PERM, where applicable) was properly filed.
2. **Confirm your country of chargeability** — this may differ from citizenship (e.g., cross-chargeability through a spouse).
3. **Compare against the current bulletin** — you can file or be approved only if your category and country are current or your priority date is earlier than the listed date.
4. **File Form I-140**, then adjust status via **Form I-485** (in the U.S.) or consular processing (abroad).
5. **Track adjudication speed.** Monitor how long I-140 petitions are taking on our [I-140 processing times page](/uscis-processing-times/i-140) and employment-based adjustment on the [I-485 (employment-based) processing times page](/uscis-processing-times/i-485-eb).

## What EB-1A applicants should prepare

EB-1A is evidence-driven. Build a record around the regulatory criteria — awards, published material about you, judging others' work, original contributions of major significance, scholarly articles, and evidence of high remuneration. Because EB-1 dates for India and China can retrogress or go unavailable quickly, having a **complete, well-documented petition ready to file** lets you move the moment a number is available.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the EB-1 green card current in 2026?
For most of the world, yes. Per the August 2026 Visa Bulletin, EB-1 is **Current** for all countries except **China (July 1, 2023)** and **India (October 15, 2022)**. Dates can change monthly.

### Can I self-petition for an EB-1A green card?
Yes. **EB-1A (extraordinary ability)** does not require a job offer or labor certification, so you can self-petition with Form I-140. EB-1B and EB-1C require an employer.

### Why might EB-1 India become unavailable?
EB-1 receives a fixed share of the annual employment-based limit. When demand from a high-volume country like India exceeds the per-country allotment late in the fiscal year, the State Department can declare the category **"Unavailable"** until the new fiscal year begins in October.

### How long does an EB-1 petition take?
It depends on the form and whether you use premium processing. Check current adjudication trends on our [I-140 processing times page](/uscis-processing-times/i-140) and the [USCIS processing times hub](/uscis-processing-times).

### What is the difference between EB-1 and EB-2?
EB-1 (first preference) is for priority workers and often moves faster; EB-2 (second preference) is for advanced-degree professionals and persons of exceptional ability and, for India, is currently **unavailable** in the August 2026 bulletin.
