# The 2025 Naturalization Civics Test: What Changed and Who Has to Take It

If you are applying for U.S. citizenship, the exam at your interview may look different than you expect. USCIS has reimplemented a longer, harder version of the civics exam — the **2025 Naturalization Civics Test** — for many applicants. This guide explains exactly what changed, the cutoff date that decides which test you take, and how to prepare.

> **Bottom line:** The **2025 Naturalization Civics Test** applies to naturalization applications filed **on or after October 20, 2025**. It uses a **bank of 128 questions**, the officer asks **20**, and you must answer **12 correctly** to pass. Applicants who filed earlier (or within 30 days of the September 18, 2025 notice) take the older **2008 test** (100-question bank, 10 asked, 6 to pass).

## What the 2025 civics test changes

The 2025 test reimplements the 2020 version (with modifications). Compared with the long-standing 2008 test, the key differences are:

| Feature | 2008 Test | 2025 Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question bank | 100 questions | **128 questions** |
| Questions asked at interview | 10 | **20** |
| Correct answers needed to pass | 6 | **12** |
| Format | Oral, officer-administered | Oral, officer-administered |
| English (reading/writing/speaking) components | Unchanged | **Unchanged** |

The officer asks questions from a computer-generated list drawn from the 128-question bank and **stops once you have answered 12 correctly** (or once it is clear you cannot reach 12). The **passing score itself was not changed** — the update is a procedural change to the content and number of questions. USCIS notes roughly **75% of the 2025 content overlaps with the 2008 test**, so much of the classic study material still applies.

## Which test will you take? The October 20, 2025 cutoff

The version you take depends on **when you filed Form N-400**:

- **Filed on or after October 20, 2025:** You take the **2025 Naturalization Civics Test** (128-bank / 20 asked / 12 to pass).
- **Filed before that date, or within 30 days of the September 18, 2025 notice:** You take the **2008 test** (100-bank / 10 asked / 6 to pass).

### Special rule for older, long-term residents

Applicants who are **65 or older** and have been lawful permanent residents for **20 or more years** continue to get a simplified exam: a smaller set of designated questions with a lower number of correct answers required. If you qualify for the 65/20 accommodation, confirm the exact question set that applies to your filing date.

## How to prepare

1. **Confirm your filing date.** It determines which test — and which study materials — apply to you.
2. **Study the correct question bank.** Use the official USCIS study materials for the 128-question set if you filed on or after October 20, 2025.
3. **Practice out loud.** The test is oral; practicing spoken answers helps with both civics and the English speaking component.
4. **Don't forget the English test.** The reading, writing, and speaking requirements are unchanged and still part of the interview.
5. **Track your case.** See how long naturalization cases are taking on our [N-400 processing times page](/uscis-processing-times/n-400) so you know when to expect your interview.

## Why this matters for timing your interview

Because most applicants are interviewed at least a few months after filing, the version of the test you take is effectively locked in by your **N-400 filing date**. If you have already filed, check your receipt date against the October 20, 2025 cutoff. If you are about to file, know that you will almost certainly take the 2025 test. Either way, keeping an eye on [current N-400 processing times](/uscis-processing-times/n-400) helps you plan your study timeline.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many questions are on the 2025 naturalization civics test?
The officer asks **20 questions** from a bank of **128**, and you must answer **12 correctly** to pass. This is up from 10 asked and 6 correct on the 2008 test.

### Who has to take the 2025 civics test?
Applicants who file Form N-400 **on or after October 20, 2025**. Those who filed earlier — or within 30 days of the September 18, 2025 Federal Register notice — take the 2008 test.

### Did the passing score change?
No. USCIS describes the change as **procedural** — the number of questions and the size of the bank increased, but the passing standard itself was not changed. You still must answer the required number correctly.

### Is the English portion of the test different?
No. The reading, writing, and speaking (English language) components are **unchanged**. Only the civics portion was updated.

### I'm 65 or older with 20+ years as a green card holder — is there an easier test?
Yes. Long-term residents who are 65 or older with 20+ years of permanent residence continue to receive a simplified civics exam with fewer questions and a lower number of correct answers required. Confirm the exact set based on your filing date.
